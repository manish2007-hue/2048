How 2048 Scores & Leaderboards Work

In 2048, score points are granted immediately whenever two numbered tiles collide and merge. The point value earned is equal to the exact number of the newly formed tile. For instance, combining two 4 tiles grants 8 points; combining two 1024 tiles adds 2048 points directly to your score tally.
