Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

At 2048 Online (play2048online.site), your privacy is of paramount importance to us. We are committed to transparency, data minimization, and user control. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we do and do not collect, how local storage is utilized, and your legal rights under modern privacy regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, or credit card details to play 2048. You can enjoy the full gaming experience, track your scores, and participate in leaderboards completely anonymously.

  • No personally identifiable information (PII) is sold or traded.
  • No cross-site fingerprinting or intrusive advertising trackers.
  • No background microphone, camera, or location permissions are requested.

3. Browser Local Storage Usage

Our application uses your browser's client-side HTML5 LocalStorage to provide core single-player functionality. This data is stored strictly on your device and is never transmitted to an external server:

●
Player Profile: Your custom nickname used on local leaderboards and match records.
●
Game Records & Statistics: Your high scores, highest tile achieved, move counts, and match history.
●
User Preferences: Your sound toggle state (on/off) and on-screen D-Pad display preferences.

You have full control over this data. You can delete it at any time by clicking "Reset History" in the Leaderboard tab, clearing your browser cache, or exporting your data via "Export Stats JSON".

4. Third-Party Services and CDNs

To maintain high performance and visual consistency, 2048 Online loads static libraries from reputable Content Delivery Networks (CDNs):

  • Google Fonts: For serving typography (Inter font family). Google may collect standard server request headers as per their privacy policy.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN: For compiling and caching lightweight stylesheets.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

2048 Online is a family-friendly mathematical puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Because our service requires no registration or personal details, it is safe for players of all ages.

6. GDPR & CCPA User Rights

If you reside in the European Union (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you enjoy fundamental rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: View all saved data directly inside the Player Dashboard.
  • Right to Portability: Export your complete match history and lifetime statistics into an open JSON format using our one-click export button.
  • Right to Erasure: Wipe all records immediately using the "Reset History" button.

7. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy compliance team via our Contact Us page or by email at privacy@play2048online.site.
