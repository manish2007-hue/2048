4. How to Play 2048 Unblocked

Never played 2048 before? Here's the quick version:

  1. Swipe or use arrow keys to slide all tiles in one direction (Up, Down, Left, Right).
  2. Matching tiles merge — when two tiles with the same number collide, they combine into one tile with double the value (2+2=4, 4+4=8, etc.).
  3. A new tile spawns after each move — either a 2 (90% chance) or a 4 (10% chance).
  4. Reach the 2048 tile to win! Then keep going toward 4096, 8192, and beyond.
  5. Game over when the board fills up with no possible merges remaining.
